## 1. Sign up

Create an account with your email and a business name. Follow the link in the verification email, which you need before you can sign in. Your workspace starts with a **14-day Premium trial** and no card is needed. When it ends, you move to Free with your data intact. Nothing is deleted; anything over the Free limits becomes read-only or unpublished.

## 2. Choose how to start

You have three routes:

- **Workspace template**: pick your kind of business and answer a few questions. Graft creates entities, sample records, bookable items and forms for you. See [Workspace templates](../../guide/templates/).
- **Guided setup**: Graft asks what you are setting up and what should happen with it, then builds the entity, your first items and a form step by step.
- **From scratch**: define an [entity](../../guide/entities/) and build a [form](../../guide/forms/) yourself. The in-app **Guide** page walks through this.

## 3. Add real data

Open an entity and use **Add record**, or [import a CSV](../../guide/records/#importing-records) on Premium. Replace any sample data a template created.

## 4. Publish a form

In **Forms**, check the fields, then **Publish**. Your form is live at:

```
/f/<your-workspace-slug>/<form-slug>
```

Share the link. Each submission lands in **Operations → Inbox** and as a record in the entity.

## 5. Run the day

**Operations** is where you work: today's arrivals and money owed, orders, customers, the inbox, a board and the schedule. See [Operations](../../guide/operations/).

## 6. Invite your team

On **Account → Team**, the owner creates an invite link. Free workspaces have one seat; Premium has 15. See [Team and seats](../../guide/team/).
